# Appendix N co-write examples (notes only)

These are Ray Weiss's own compositions, notes only, licensed under **CC BY 4.0**. Credit: **Ray Weiss**.

Each `.notes.xrns` is a Renoise song zip that holds only `Song.xml`. The original `.xrns` files also held
third-party samples (BBC Symphony Orchestra, LABS, and drum one-shots); those `SampleData/` members are
removed here. Renoise opens these notes-only files with empty instruments.

## Which file is which piece

From appendix-n `docs/handoffs/an-music-s332/return/REPORT.md`, section "Which files are Ray's two pieces":

> **Piece 1 = ANfirstdecent, with ANattempt1 and anexperiment as its drafts.** ANfirstdecent and anexperiment
> share two exact chord voicings by pitch class, A-C-E-B (Am add9) and F-C-E-B (Fmaj7#11). anexperiment plays
> them as its patterns 2-3, and ANfirstdecent opens with them as its patterns 0-1. The two files also share the
> BBC harp sample, the 75 BPM tempo and the 256-line length. ANattempt1 and anexperiment were saved 1 h 49 min
> apart on 21 July, both on the chipsynth SFC chord instrument, and both open on a C-E-G-B (Cmaj7) stack in
> C major. ANattempt1 also shares the C-D-E-A cluster with ANfirstdecent. The name "first decent" reads as
> Ray's own label for the one that worked.
>
> **Piece 2 = maybean2** ("maybe AN 2"). It has a different key (D major over a D pedal, G | Bm7 | Gmaj7 |
> D/F#), different instruments, and the only drums in the set.
